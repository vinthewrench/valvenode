Valve Node Example Commands
===========================

Assumptions:
- Node address = 1
- Checksum checking is currently ignored
- Frame format: @|LEN|COMMAND|ADDR|CHECKSUM<CR>

Paste these into your terminal.

Basic commands
--------------

PING
@|6|PING|1|00

STATUS
@|8|STATUS|1|00

OPEN
@|6|OPEN|1|00

CLOSE
@|7|CLOSE|1|00

WHO broadcast
@|7|WHO|255|00


Expected replies
----------------

PING reply
@|5|ACK|1|xx

STATUS reply when CLOSED
@|14|STATE|1|CLOSED|xx

STATUS reply when OPEN
@|12|STATE|1|OPEN|xx

WHO reply
@|4|ID|1|xx


Commands with real checksums
----------------------------

PING
@|6|PING|1|D2

STATUS
@|8|STATUS|1|57

OPEN
@|6|OPEN|1|E0

CLOSE
@|7|CLOSE|1|4F

WHO broadcast
@|7|WHO|255|0D


Suggested bench test order
--------------------------

@|8|STATUS|1|00

@|8|STATUS|1|00

Terminal note
-------------

If your terminal sends LF instead of CR, the parser may ignore the line.
